About Our Jewellery Brand

Crafting Timeless Gold & Diamond Jewellery

We are a fine jewellery brand specializing in handcrafted gold, diamond, and precious gemstone jewellery—blending traditional craftsmanship with modern elegance for timeless creations.

Every creation at New Pratima Jewellers begins with a promise — uncompromised purity, ethical sourcing, and masterful artistry refined over generations.

Rooted in tradition yet inspired by contemporary elegance, our collections range from everyday classics to exquisite bridal masterpieces, designed to celebrate life’s most meaningful moments.

With transparent pricing, BIS-hallmarked gold, certified diamonds, and generations of loyal patrons, we continue to uphold values that families trust and cherish.

  • ✦ BIS Hallmarked Gold & Certified Diamonds
  • ✦ Ethical Sourcing & Transparent Pricing
  • ✦ Bespoke Bridal Jewellery & Custom Creations
  • ✦ Trusted by Generations of Families
